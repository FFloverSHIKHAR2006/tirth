Namisharanya Yatra

Journey to the sacred forest where sages received divine knowledge

Pilgrimage Route

📍

Route Information

Located in Sitapur district of Uttar Pradesh, the yatra covers the sacred Chakratirtha and 88 holy spots.

🙏

Significance

Where 88,000 rishis performed the famous Satra yajna and where Ved Vyasa narrated the Puranas.

📅

Best Time

Phalgun month (February-March) during the Maha Shivratri period is ideal for this pilgrimage.
