84 Kos Yatra

The sacred 84 Kos Parikrama around Mathura-Vrindavan

Pilgrimage Route

📍

Route Information

The parikrama covers 252 km around Mathura-Vrindavan, passing through 12 sacred forests and numerous temples.

🙏

Significance

This yatra recreates Lord Krishna's divine pastimes and offers liberation to devotees who complete it with devotion.

📅

Best Time

Kartik month (October-November) is considered most auspicious for this sacred journey.
